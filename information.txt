About Neelaxmi:
Neelaxmi is the community platform behind this site, built by Team Saksen to bring Ajay Sir (AKM Sir)'s Physical Chemistry masterclasses, quizzes, PYQ series and study material together in one place for NEET and JEE aspirants. It hosts free daily classes, topic-wise course modules, previous year question banks (the "J* Series"), live quizzes and downloadable PDFs.

About Us:
We are a student-run team building free, structured resources so learners can revise with clarity instead of scattered notes. Our goal is honest, concept-first teaching — no paywalled fundamentals, just daily classes, PYQ-tested modules and doubt-solving built for students who actually want to understand the subject, not just memorise it.
